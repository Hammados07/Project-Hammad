# 📘 Step-by-Step Playbook: Content, Books & Freelancing

Platform rules and thresholds change often. Double-check the current requirements on each platform's official help page before relying on a number.

---

## A. YouTube Channel

### Phase 1: Set up (Week 1)
1. **Pick the niche and one-line promise.** Example: *"I help ops & BPO people become data analysts using Excel, SQL and Power BI."*
2. **Create a brand name** (my name, or something like "The RTA Desk" / "Analyst Shift"). Check it's free on YouTube and Instagram.
3. Create the channel with a separate Google account. Add banner, logo (Canva), about section and email for business enquiries.
4. **Research:** Find 10 channels in the niche. Note their most-viewed videos, titles and thumbnails. Look for topics with demand but weak explanations.
5. Make a **list of 30 video ideas** in a sheet. Mix three types:
   - *Searchable tutorials*: "XLOOKUP vs VLOOKUP", "SQL window functions explained", "Erlang C calculator in Excel"
   - *Career*: "How I became a Real-Time Analyst", "RTA interview questions", "BPO to Data Analyst roadmap"
   - *Projects*: "I built a call-centre dashboard in Power BI" (straight from this repo)

### Phase 2: Produce (weekly loop)
| Step | Time | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Script / outline | 45 min | Hook in the first 15 seconds: say what they'll get. Bullet outline is enough for tutorials |
| Record | 45–60 min | Screen record with OBS plus face-cam or voice. Record in chunks |
| Edit | 1.5–2 hrs | Cut silences, add zooms and captions. Don't over-edit early videos |
| Thumbnail + title | 30 min | 3–4 words on the thumbnail, clear face/emotion, high contrast |
| Upload + SEO | 20 min | Keyword in title, 2–3 line description, chapters, 5–8 tags, pinned comment |

**Target: 1 long video (8–15 min) per week + 2–3 Shorts cut from it.**

### Phase 3: Grow & monetise
- **Consistency beats quality at the start.** Aim for 30 videos before judging results.
- Every month, check YouTube Studio: CTR (aim for over 4–5%), average view duration, which videos bring subscribers. Make more of what works.
- **YouTube Partner Program:** Has historically required 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days) for ad revenue, with a lower tier for fan-funding features. Check current rules.
- **Income sources beyond ads (often bigger):** affiliate links (courses, books, gear), selling my own eBook or Excel templates, sponsorships, 1-to-1 mentoring/resume review, freelancing leads.

## B. Instagram (two tracks)

### Track 1: Data/Analyst page (repurposed from YouTube)
1. Switch to a **Professional (Creator) account** and use the same name as YouTube.
2. Bio: who I help + what they get + link (use a free link-in-bio page).
3. Post **3–4 times a week**:
   - Reels: 30–60 sec cut from YouTube videos (one tip per reel)
   - Carousels: "5 Excel shortcuts every RTA needs", "SQL JOINs cheat-sheet"
   - Stories: daily polls/questions, which build engagement
4. Spend 15 minutes a day replying to comments and commenting on 5–10 accounts in the niche.

### Track 2: Poetry page (creative, separate handle or same page later)
1. Choose a pen name / style (Urdu–Hindi shayari, English free verse, or bilingual).
2. Post **3 poems a week**: text-on-image (Canva template for a consistent look) and Reels of me reciting over soft music.
3. Use a consistent template, font and colour palette so the page looks like a brand.
4. Save every poem in one document (see Poetry Book below). **This page becomes my book's test market:** poems that get the most saves/shares go in the book.

## C. Poetry Book

1. **Write consistently:** 3–4 poems a week. After 6 months I'll have ~80–100.
2. **Curate:** Pick the best 50–70 using Instagram saves/shares as feedback. Group into 3–5 themed sections (love, loss, hope, city life, faith…).
3. **Edit:** Get 2–3 trusted readers to review. Then do a final proofread.
4. **Format:** Use Canva, Google Docs or Kindle Create (free) for the interior. Get a cover made (Canva or a freelancer for ₹1–3k).
5. **Publish (free options):**
   - **Amazon KDP**: eBook + paperback (print-on-demand, no stock needed)
   - **Notion Press / Pothi**: Indian self-publishing with print-on-demand and distribution
6. **Launch:** Countdown posts on Instagram for 2 weeks, share poems from the book, ask the first readers for reviews.

> Poetry books rarely make big money. Treat this as a **brand and passion** asset: it builds my identity as a writer and supports future books.

## D. Explanatory Book (e.g. *"Excel, SQL & Power BI for Real-Time Analysts"*)

**Start this first of the three books.** It's easiest to sell because it solves a real problem, and it's built from the projects and videos I'm already making.

1. **Define the reader:** e.g. *"a BPO/ops employee who wants to become an MIS/RTA/data analyst."*
2. **Outline 10–15 chapters** that match my upskill timetable (Excel → SQL → Power BI → WFM metrics → interview prep).
3. **Write 1 chapter per week** (~2,000–3,000 words). Reuse my video scripts. Include screenshots, exercises and practice datasets from this repo.
4. **Beta readers:** Share a free PDF of 2–3 chapters with my audience and ask for feedback. This also collects emails.
5. **Publish:**
   - Amazon KDP (eBook + paperback)
   - Also sell as a **PDF + practice files bundle** via Gumroad / Topmate / Instamojo. These can earn a better cut than a bookstore.
6. **Price:** ₹199–₹499 for the eBook. Premium bundle (book + Excel templates + SQL scripts) at ₹699–₹999.

---

## E. Self-Help Book (later, 12+ months)

This genre is crowded, and readers buy from people they trust. So:
1. **Build credibility first.** My story (e.g. *"From BPO shift to data analyst while writing poetry"*) becomes the angle.
2. **Keep a journal from today** of lessons, routines and failures. This becomes the raw material.
3. Test ideas as Instagram carousels/threads. Topics that get strong engagement become chapters.
4. Write when I have an audience of a few thousand who already know my story. Publish the same way as above.

---

## F. Freelancing (fastest cash)

1. **Package 3 services** I can deliver in evenings/weekends:
   - Excel automation / cleanup / dashboards (₹1,500–₹5,000 per task to start)
   - Power BI dashboard from client data (₹5,000–₹20,000)
   - SQL queries / reports / data cleaning
2. **Portfolio:** Link the projects in this repo plus 2–3 sample dashboards (with synthetic data).
3. **Where to find clients:** Fiverr, Upwork, LinkedIn posts, local small businesses (shops, clinics, coaching centres need simple sales/stock reports), and later my own YouTube/Instagram audience.
4. Start with lower prices to get **5 reviews**, then raise prices every 5 clients.
5. ⚠️ Check the moonlighting clause in my employment contract first (see the main README).
