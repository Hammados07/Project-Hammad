# 🗓️ Schedule & Timetable

**Budget:** ~**15–17 hours/week** on top of the job (≈1.5 hrs on weekdays, 4–5 hrs on Saturday, 2–3 hrs on Sunday). This is sustainable. More than that usually leads to burnout.

> **RTA shifts rotate a lot.** The template below assumes a **day shift (9:30 AM – 6:30 PM)**. For a night or evening shift, keep the *order of blocks* and shift the clock: the **deep-work block** always goes in the first 2 hours after I wake up properly, when my brain is fresh, and the **light work** (engagement, reading) goes after the shift.

---

## 1. Daily template (weekday, day shift)

| Time | Block | Activity |
|---|---|---|

| **20:30 – 21:00** | ✍️ **Light creative (30 min)** | Poetry writing / Instagram engagement / plan tomorrow |
| 21:00 – 22:30 | 📴 Wind down | Reading, family, no work |
| 22:30 | 😴 Sleep | **7.5–8 hrs. Non-negotiable.** Everything else fails without sleep |

## 2. Weekly theme (so every day has one clear job)

| Day | Morning deep work (60 min) | Evening light (30 min) |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | 📚 Upskill: learn new topic | ✍️ Write 1 poem |
| **Tue** | 📚 Upskill: practice exercises | 📝 Outline this week's YouTube video |
| **Wed** | 🛠️ Repo project: build | ✍️ Write 1 poem / post poetry |
| **Thu** | 📚 Upskill: practice + notes | 📝 Finish script for video |
| **Fri** | 🛠️ Repo project: finish + commit + README | 💬 Instagram engagement, rest early |
| **Sat** (4–5 hrs) | 🎥 **9:00–11:00** Record video · **11:30–13:30** Edit · *afternoon off* | ✍️ Book chapter (1 hr, from month 3) |
| **Sun** (2–3 hrs) | 🎬 **10:00–11:00** Thumbnail, upload, cut 2–3 Shorts/Reels · **11:00–11:30** Schedule Instagram posts · **11:30–12:00** 📊 Weekly review | 🧘 Rest, family, plan the week |

**Weekly output target:** 3 upskill sessions · 1 repo project step · 1 YouTube video · 2–3 Shorts/Reels · 3 poems · (from month 3) 1 book chapter.

### Weekly review (Sunday, 30 min)
1. What did I finish? (update the Task 5 tracker: hours, posts, income, expenses)
2. What didn't happen, and why?
3. Top-performing post/video this week: what can I repeat?
4. Plan next week: pick the video topic, upskill topic, project step.

## 5. Rules to keep this sustainable
1. **Never skip twice.** Missing one day is fine. Missing two becomes a habit.
2. **One rest day minimum** (Sunday afternoon/evening is sacred).
3. **Batch work:** record 2 videos on a good Saturday so a bad week doesn't break consistency.
4. **During high-pressure weeks at work** (peak season, shift changes), drop to the *minimum*: 1 Short + 2 poems + 2 upskill sessions.
5. **Review monthly, not daily.** Views and followers swing a lot day to day. Look at trends over 4 weeks.
