# 🎥 YouTube Teaching Kit: Learn It, Then Teach It

Your study plan and your channel are **the same thing**. Every module = at least one episode.

---

## 1. The series

**Series name ideas:** *"MBA in AI & Data Science: Explained Like You're 5"* · *"The 48-Week MBA Rebuild"* · *"Analyst to AI: MBA Simplified"*

**Why it can work:**
- A clear promise (the whole MBA, simply) + a **journey** people can follow week by week ("learn with me").
- Your unique angle: **a Real-Time Analyst in a contact centre**, using real-world (synthetic) ops examples that most channels don't have.
- It's a natural playlist, and later the **explanatory book** (see `growth-plan/01-content-and-books-playbook.md`).

**Playlists = semesters:**
1. Semester 1: Business basics + Stats + Python + SQL
2. Semester 2: Marketing, Finance, Ops, Stats II, Regression, R, Power BI
3. Semester 3: Machine Learning, Forecasting, Big Data
4. Semester 4: Advanced AI, GenAI, Strategy, Capstone
5. Side playlist: **Call Centre Analytics** (Erlang C, forecasting, SLA, Power BI dashboards) ← your niche

## 2. Episode script template (8–12 minutes)

Use the module's 8 parts directly:

| Time | Section | What you say / show | Source in module |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | **Hook** | A question or surprising fact: *"Your company's average salary is lying to you."* | 🎥 Teach it |
| 0:20–0:40 | **Promise** | "By the end you'll be able to ___." | 🎯 Key concepts |
| 0:40–2:00 | **Like I'm 5** | The analogy, with a simple drawing or props | 🧒 Like I'm 5 |
| 2:00–6:00 | **Key concepts** | 3–5 ideas, one on-screen text card each | 🎯 Key concepts |
| 6:00–7:00 | **Memory trick** | The mnemonic | 🧠 Remember it |
| 7:00–10:00 | **Do it live** | Excel / Python / SQL screen recording on the lab | 🛠️ Build it |
| 10:00–10:45 | **Business "so what"** | Where companies use this, in ₹ | business-acumen.md |
| 10:45–11:30 | **Quiz + CTA** | Ask 1 self-test question: "answer in the comments". Next episode teaser | ✅ Self-test |

**Recording tips:** Screen + face-cam (OBS). Script bullet points only, never read word-for-word. Record the "Do it live" part first (hardest), and the intro last (you'll be warmed up).

**Every episode also produces:**
- 2–3 **Shorts/Reels** (the analogy, the mnemonic, one quick tip)
- 1 **Instagram carousel** (your one-page summary)
- 1 **LinkedIn post** ("This week I relearned ___. Here's the simplest explanation.")
- 1 **book chapter draft** (the script + notes, cleaned up)

## 4. Rules for teaching (so the channel stays trustworthy)
1. **Only teach after you've built it.** If you can't do the lab, you're not ready to record.
2. **Say "I was wrong" in public** when a comment corrects you. Pin the correction. It builds trust.
3. **Never show company data.** All examples use the synthetic datasets in `labs/data/`.
4. **Cite your sources** in the description (the NPTEL course, the book).
5. **Consistency > perfection:** 1 episode a week for a year beats 10 perfect ones and silence.
