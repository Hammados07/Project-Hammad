# 🧭 Business Acumen: Thinking Like an MBA Graduate

> 🧒 **Like I'm 5:** Business acumen is **seeing a company the way its owner does**: *"How does this make money? What could go wrong? What would I do next?"* Tools (Python, SQL) get you hired. Business acumen gets you **promoted**.

This runs **alongside all 4 semesters**. About 1.5 hours a week, mostly on the commute.

---

## 1. Weekly habits

| Habit | Time | How |
|---|---|---|
| 📰 **Read business news like a CEO** | 15 min/day | *Finshots* (free daily newsletter), *Mint* / *Economic Times* headlines. For each story ask: **Who wins? Who loses? What's the number?** |
| 📊 **One company teardown per month** | 2 hrs/month | Use the template below |
| 🎧 **One business podcast episode per week** | commute | *Acquired*, *The Seen and the Unseen* (Amit Varma), *Finshots Daily* |
| 🧮 **One guesstimate per week** | 10 min | "How many Uber rides happen in Bengaluru daily?" |
| 💬 **Translate your work to ₹** | ongoing | "SLA improved 5%" → "fewer abandoned calls → X more resolved customers → ₹Y retained revenue" |

---

## 2. The monthly company teardown (1-page template)

1. **What does it sell, and to whom?** (Business Model Canvas, M1)
2. **How does it make money?** Revenue streams and the biggest one.
3. **The numbers** (annual report / screener.in / Zerodha Varsity method, M2):
   revenue growth · operating margin · net margin · ROE · debt-to-equity · cash from operations
4. **Competition:** Five Forces + the moat (M26).
5. **Risks:** what could kill it in 5 years?
6. **If I were CEO,** my one move would be… (and the number that supports it).

## 4. Business glossary: 60 terms, explained like you're 5

### Money & Accounting
| Term | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| Revenue / Top line | All the money that came in from sales |
| Profit / Bottom line | What's left after paying for everything |
| Gross margin | % left after paying only for making the product |
| EBITDA | Profit before interest, tax, depreciation & amortisation, i.e. "how good is the core business" |
| Depreciation | Spreading an asset's cost over the years you use it |
| Asset / Liability / Equity | What you own / what you owe / what's truly yours |
| Working capital | Cash tied up in day-to-day running |
| Cash burn | How fast a start-up spends its cash |
| Runway | Months left before cash runs out |
| Fixed vs variable cost | Rent (same every month) vs raw material (grows with sales) |
| Break-even | The point where you stop losing money |
| Unit economics | Profit or loss on **one** customer/order |
| Capex vs Opex | Big long-term purchases vs everyday running costs |

### Finance & Investing
| Term | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| Time value of money | ₹100 today > ₹100 next year |
| NPV | Today's value of future money minus the cost. Positive = good |
| IRR | The "interest rate" a project earns |
| WACC / cost of capital | The minimum return investors expect |
| ROI / ROE / ROCE | Return on investment / on shareholders' money / on all capital used |
| Valuation | What a company is worth |
| Market cap | Share price × number of shares |
| P/E ratio | How many years of profit you pay for when you buy a share |
| Dividend | Profit shared with owners |
| Leverage | Using borrowed money to grow (grows gains *and* losses) |
| Diversification | Not keeping all your eggs in one basket |
| Beta | How much a stock swings compared to the market |
| Liquidity | How quickly you can turn something into cash |
| SIP | Investing a fixed amount every month |

### Marketing & Sales
| Term | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| Value proposition | Why a customer should pick you |
| Segmentation / Targeting / Positioning | Split customers, choose some, own a place in their mind |
| CAC | Cost to win one new customer |
| CLV / LTV | Total profit from one customer over their lifetime |
| Churn | % of customers who leave |
| Conversion rate | % of visitors who buy |
| Funnel | Steps from "heard of you" to "bought" |
| Brand equity | Extra money people pay for your name |
| Market share | Your slice of the total market pie |
| TAM / SAM / SOM | Total market / the part you can serve / the part you can realistically win |
| Go-to-market (GTM) | Plan for launching and selling |
| NPS | "Would you recommend us?" score |

### Operations & Strategy
| Term | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| KPI | The few numbers that tell you if you're winning |
| SLA | A promise of service level (e.g. 80% of calls answered in 20 sec) |
| Bottleneck | The slowest step that limits everything |
| Capacity / Utilisation | How much you can do / how much you're doing |
| Economies of scale | Bigger → cheaper per unit |
| Lean / Six Sigma | Remove waste / reduce errors |
| Supply chain | Everyone involved from raw material to customer |
| Competitive advantage / moat | What protects you from competitors |
| Network effect | Product gets better as more people use it |
| Disruption | A cheaper/simpler product that eventually beats the leaders |
| Synergy | 1 + 1 = 3 after a merger (in theory) |
| Stakeholder | Anyone affected by the company |
| Opportunity cost | The value of what you gave up |
| Sunk cost | Money already gone. Ignore it in decisions |
| MVP | The simplest version of a product that tests the idea |
| OKR | Objectives & Key Results: goal-setting method |
| Benchmarking | Comparing yourself to the best |
| Scalability | Can it grow without costs growing as fast? |
| Pivot | Changing business direction based on what you learned |
| Moonlighting | Doing a second job outside your main employment. Check your contract! |
| Due diligence | Careful checking before a big decision or deal |

## 5. Speaking "boss language" (translate analyst → manager)

| Analyst says | Manager hears better |
|---|---|
| "The model's AUC is 0.84" | "We can catch 7 out of 10 customers about to leave, before they leave" |
| "AHT went up 22 seconds" | "We need ~4 more agents per shift, or ₹X/month, unless we fix the cause" |
| "p < 0.05" | "This improvement is real, not luck" |
| "Occupancy is 92%" | "Agents are close to burnout; attrition risk is rising" |
| "Forecast MAPE is 6%" | "Our staffing plan is usually within 6% of reality" |

**Rule:** every number you present should end with **"…so we should ___."**
